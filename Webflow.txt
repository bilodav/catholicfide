# Article Structure
**Every article could follow the same pattern so readers know what to expect.**

For example:

1. The Question
- "Did Jesus ever claim to be God?"
2. The Short Answer
- A 2–3 paragraph summary for people who don't want a long read.
3. The Biblical Evidence
- Relevant passages with explanations.
4. Historical Evidence
- Early Christian writings, historians, archaeology, etc.
5. Common Objections
- Address the strongest opposing arguments fairly.
6. Catholic Perspective
- Explain how the Church understands the issue.
7. Further Reading
- Scripture references.
- Church Fathers.
- Catechism references.
- Books and articles.
